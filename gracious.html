Topic About me

I'm Loyo Gracious

I like exploring new places, taking photos and finding the right music for the journey. I don't need to travel far to feel like an explorer; a different route through a familiar place can be enough.

Session 2 · Travel & photography
Topic Places and photography

A good day starts with a turn you haven't taken

I take the long way, look for details I might usually miss, and save photos of the in-between moments. A busy market, a bright wall or a new place to sit can turn a regular afternoon into an adventure.

Session 3 · Reflection
Topic Lessons from exploring

Look around before you rush on

Exploring has taught me to stay open to surprises and appreciate what is close to home. I have learned that a good story is often less about how far you go and more about what you notice along the way.

My travel kit

  • A camera (or a phone)
  • A playlist for the ride
  • Time to take the scenic route
